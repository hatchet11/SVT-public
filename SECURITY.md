# Security Policy — Omega Point Solutions LLC

Omega Point Solutions builds software for public-safety, law-enforcement, and
government customers. We take vulnerability reports seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability
Email **security@omegapointsolutions.com** (or Crose@omegapointsolutions.com).
Include steps to reproduce and impact. Please do not open public issues for
security matters. We acknowledge reports within 3 business days and aim to
provide a remediation timeline within 10 business days.

## Scope
These public repositories are project overviews and marketing; production
source is private. Reports about our hosted products (fraudtrax.net and
omegapointsolutions.com subdomains) are in scope.

## Coordinated disclosure
We support coordinated disclosure and will credit reporters who wish to be named.
